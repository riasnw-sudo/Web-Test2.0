What is Model United Nations?

Model United Nations (MUN) is an academic simulation of the United Nations where participants assume the roles of diplomats representing different countries.

Delegates research their assigned country’s policies, engage in formal debates on global issues, negotiate with other delegates, and draft and pass resolutions.

MUN provides a practical and immersive learning experience in international relations, diplomacy, and public speaking.

IIUMMUN is hosted by the International Islamic University Malaysia and brings together students from diverse backgrounds. It is open to secondary school and university students, first-timers and experienced delegates, local and international. The conference is inclusive while maintaining high academic and debate standards.

Terms and conditions

Delegate eligibility

The conference welcomes everyone with a strong interest in global affairs or prior MUN experience. Delegates may indicate up to three council preferences and three country allocations. Final assignments are determined by the secretariat.

Observer and advisor eligibility

Observers may be of any age and may include parents, guardians, mentors, or siblings of delegates who wish to experience the conference without directly participating in committee sessions.

Teacher eligibility

Teachers may be of any age and wish to experience the conference without directly participating in committee sessions.
