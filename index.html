What is Model United Nations?

Model United Nations (MUN) is an academic simulation of the United Nations where participants assume the roles of diplomats representing different countries.

Delegates research their assigned country’s policies, engage in formal debates on global issues, negotiate with other delegates, and draft and pass resolutions.

MUN provides a practical and immersive learning experience in international relations, diplomacy, and public speaking.

IIUMMUN is open to secondary school and university students, first-timers and experienced delegates, local and international. The conference is inclusive while maintaining high academic and debate standards.

Secretariat room

IRK LR 2-7. Delegates, observers, advisors and guests can ask here about attendance, the schedule, directions to council rooms, general inquiries and emergencies. The Secretariat can also give first aid for minor injuries.

Observers and advisors’ room

IRK LR 2-8. A resting and waiting area with seating, tables, charging points (subject to availability) and a luggage area. Label your belongings and keep valuables with you. IIUMMUNC’ 2026 and the Secretariat are not responsible for loss, theft or damage.

Medical services

Report any injury or medical concern to the nearest Secretariat member. If no one is nearby, go to the Secretariat Room. For anything beyond basic first aid, delegates may go to the IIUM Clinic with a Secretariat member, or to a nearby medical facility outside IIUM. Tell your advisor if you have one.

Accessibility

Tell the Secretariat about any accessibility needs before the conference if you can, or approach the nearest Secretariat member on the day. Help can include guidance to accessible entrances, council rooms and seating. Availability is confirmed by the Logistics and Operations Department and the university.

    FAQ

    When and where is the conference?

    4 to 6 December 2026 at the International Islamic University Malaysia, Gombak campus.

    Who can take part?

    Secondary school students, university students, first-time participants, experienced MUN delegates, and local and international students.

    How do I register?

    Use the official Google Forms listed on the Register section. Registering through the forms keeps everyone counted in one place.

    How much does it cost?

    See the Fees section. Prices depend on your ticket type and whether you choose full board or half board.

    How do I get to IIUM?

    Take the Kelana Jaya LRT to LRT Gombak, then the T200 feeder bus to the SL155 UIAM (Selatan) stop. Details are in Logistics.

    How are awards decided?

    Awards are given by council and at the discretion of the chairs.

    Can observers and advisors attend?

    Yes. Observers and advisors have their own room (IRK LR 2-8) and a dedicated session on Day 2, 9:00 to 11:00 AM.

    Who do I contact?

    Message @iiummunc26 on Instagram or @iiummun26 on TikTok, or email iiummunconfgeneral@gmail.com.

    Terms and conditions

    Delegate eligibility

    The conference welcomes everyone with a strong interest in global affairs or prior MUN experience. Delegates may indicate up to three council preferences and three country allocations. Final assignments are determined by the secretariat.

    Observer and advisor eligibility

    Observers may be of any age and may include parents, guardians, mentors, or siblings of delegates who wish to experience the conference without directly participating in council sessions.

    Teacher eligibility

    Teachers may be of any age and wish to experience the conference without directly participating in council sessions.
